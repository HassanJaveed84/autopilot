## 6. New defects (diagnosed and documented; not fixed, no rerun)

### N1. Attribute blocks containing parentheses are not parsed (confirmed)

**Symptom.** On all three EVIDENCE lines, `kind`, `origin` and `asof` were lost. The whole line, attribute block included, became the evidence row's `excerpt`, which the evidence sheet shows as "Claude's summary". The claim's reason fell back from "The issuing source, in scope" to "3 sources from separate origins".

**Root cause.**
- `EvidenceAttrs.split` reads the leading block with `^\s*\(([^()]*)\)\s*`, which cannot match a block containing a parenthesis. All three blocks contained one:
  - the operator: `origin=Union Station D.C. management (Union Station Redevelopment Corp.)` and `asof=2026 (page about 168 days old at retrieval)`;
  - Amtrak: `asof=2025 (page about 285 days old at retrieval)`;
  - Destination DC: `origin=Washington, DC's official tourism organisation (Destination DC) listing`.

  So every line fell through to "no attributes".
- `EvidenceDisplay.summary` shows the excerpt without `LeakageScan.clean`, so the residue reached the screen.

**Reproduction.** A replay of this run's line through `EvidenceAttrs.split` gives `kind=null origin=null asOf=null`, and `LeakageScan.scan` of the displayed summary reports `kind=primary`, `origin=Union` and `asof=2026`. The same line without nested parentheses parses correctly (kind PRIMARY, origin Amtrak, asof 2025). This was a temporary probe; it is not committed.

**Direction of risk.** Losing `kind` and `origin` can only lower a status. A corroborated claim would lose its issuer route and its known independence, and an `about=` mismatch label would also be lost, which could let off-scope evidence count. That last effect is the unsafe direction. It did not occur here, because no line had `about=`.

**Proposed fix (needs the owner's approval).**
- Parse the leading block with balanced parentheses, and split values on `·` only at depth 0.
- Pass displayed summaries through `LeakageScan.clean` as defence in depth.
- In the prompt, keep attribute values to plain words and put dates in `YYYY` / `YYYY-MM` form.
- Add a regression test replaying this run's reply.

### N2. A call's search allowance described as the investigation's limit (confirmed, minor)

**Symptom.** "The search allowance was one search" in the unresolved section, while the turn was authorized for 3 searches and stopped because the fact was settled.

**Root cause.** The GATHER prompt says "Searches allowed on this call: 1" (the stage's share of the budget) and does not say that the investigation can continue if a load-bearing claim stays open. The call-narration guard applies only to calls after the first.

**Proposed fix.** The GATHER prompt says the investigation may search again if a load-bearing claim stays open, and that this call's allowance is not to be described as the investigation's limit. The composer could also treat per-call allowance wording as call narration in every call.

### Model signal: dates from page age (finding H persists)

`asof=` on the claim and on the lines came from the search results' page age, not from any date the page states. The prompt asks for "the date the page gives". This decides nothing for a stable fact. It still matters for volatile facts, which R-C and R-K exercise.
